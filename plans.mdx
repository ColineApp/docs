Prices are on the [pricing page](https://coline.app/pricing). This page explains what the plans include.

## Personal plans

For your own account and personal workspace.

| | Free | Pro | Pro + AI | Max |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kairo credits per month | 100 | 750 | 1,500 | 10,000 |
| Code credits per month | None | 2,000 | 4,000 | 10,000 |
| Storage | 20 GB | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Publish to the web | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |

Free never asks for a card. Pro starts with a 14-day trial. You can change or cancel a plan any time; changes are prorated.

## Team plans

For team workspaces, billed per member.

| | Workspace | Workspace Pro + AI | Enterprise |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kairo credits per member per month | 750 | 1,500 | Custom |
| Code credits per member per month | 2,000 | 4,000 | 10,000 |
| Storage | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Upstream repository hosting | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Single sign-on, directory sync, custom limits | | | Yes |

Workspace Pro + AI isn't available to buy yet.

Kairo credits in a team workspace go into one shared bank for the whole team. Admins can cap how much each [crew member](/kairo/crew) uses.

Team workspaces with an active Coline subscription include Upstream Team.

### Team trial

A new team workspace gets a 14-day trial without a card:

- up to 5 people, counting the owner and pending invites
- 1,500 Kairo credits shared across the trial
- everything else in the Workspace plan

When the trial ends without billing, the workspace pauses. Everything stays readable, but nothing new can be created, and invites are off, until the owner adds a payment method.

## Credits

**Kairo credits** pay for AI work across Coline: chat, drafting, summaries, research, routines and crew. A standard request costs one credit; some models and higher reasoning levels cost more, and lighter ones cost less. Credits reset at the start of each billing period. When you run out, Kairo tells you and offers an upgrade.

**Code credits** pay for cloud computers: [Code](/code) sessions, and [Kairo's computer](/kairo/computer) when it works in a browser, terminal or desktop.

**API credits** are a separate prepaid balance for metered API calls such as [Tab](/api/tab). Add them in the [developer console](https://coline.app/developers/console).

## Programs

Students, open-source maintainers and early-stage startups can apply for free or discounted access. Sign in and go to [coline.app/programs](https://coline.app/programs).
