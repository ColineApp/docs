Inbox brings notifications, messages and mail into one list so you can clear them in one pass.

## Sections

| Section | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| New | Everything you haven't dealt with |
| Mentions | Messages and comments that mention you |
| Conversations | Channels, DMs and threads with new activity |
| Later | Items you saved for later |
| Following | Threads and items you follow |
| Archive | Items you've cleared |

Filter by source, or search everything with `⌘K`.

## Triage

Save for later, snooze, archive, and mark read or unread from the keyboard, with undo. The peek pane shows an item without leaving the list.

The Inbox tab in the sidebar is a compact view of the same list. Marking everything read clears the badge, including mail.

## Smart interruptions

Coline only pings you for messages that matter. Small talk and reactions wait quietly in your Inbox, while anything urgent or meant for you still comes through. It's on by default; turn it off in Message settings. See [Messages](/messages#notifications).
