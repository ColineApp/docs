Home is built around your day. It shows:

- **Your brief:** a short summary of what changed and what matters today, written by Kairo. It doesn't repeat the cards below it.
- **Up next:** your next meetings and deadlines.
- **The day ribbon:** your meetings and free time across the day.
- **In motion:** work that's moving across your tasks, docs, code and conversations.
- **Waiting on you:** reviews, questions, approvals and replies that need you, including anything your [crew](/kairo/crew) is waiting on.
- **Your crew:** what each crew member is working on.

## Capture

Create a task or an event straight from Home without opening another app.

## Ask Kairo

One-tap actions such as **Plan my day** and **What's on my plate this week?** open Kairo with the question ready.

## Customize

Choose which sections appear on Home from its menu.
