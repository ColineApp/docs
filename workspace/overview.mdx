A workspace holds everything a group works on: conversations, files, tasks, calendars, code and apps, with one set of members and permissions.

## Personal and team workspaces

- Every account has a **personal workspace** that only its owner can see.
- **Team workspaces** are for working with other people. Create one from the workspace switcher; it starts with a [14-day trial](/plans#team-trial).

Switch workspaces from the switcher in the sidebar. Your account, profile and some memory follow you between workspaces; everything else stays in its workspace.

## Members and roles

Invite people in **Settings → Members**, by email or with an invite link. Invites wait until they're accepted, revoked or expire.

Each member has a role. Owner, Admin, Member and Guest are built in, and admins can create **custom roles** with their own permissions in **Settings → Roles and permissions**. See [permissions](/workspace/permissions).

Removing someone takes away their access right away. Their messages and files stay in the workspace.

## Teams

**Settings → Teams** groups people by what they work on. Each team gets its own drive and channels, and everyone in the team can open them. Removing someone from a team removes their access to its drive and channels, but they stay in the workspace.

## Settings

| Section | For |
| --- | --- |
| **General** | Name, logo, description, URL and custom emoji |
| **Members** | Members, invites and roles |
| **Teams** | Teams and their drives and channels |
| **Roles and permissions** | Built-in and custom roles, exceptions, and the audit log |
| **External sharing** | Who can share channels with other companies, and which domains |
| **Mail** | Workspace domains, mailboxes, aliases and routing |
| **Crew** | Team crew members, budgets and routines, and pausing the team crew |
| **Integrations** | Services connected for the team and which crew members can use them |
| **Memory** | Whether Kairo learns from conversations, and team notes |
| **Apps** | Installed apps and app policies |
| **API keys** | Keys for the [REST API](/api) |
| **Import** | Bring data from other tools; see [import](/workspace/import) |
| **Plan and billing** | Plan, seats, invoices and payment methods |
| **Code credits** | Usage of cloud computers |

Who sees which sections depends on their role.

## Billing and seats

Team plans are billed per seat. Members and pending invites each take a seat; when every seat is taken, add seats before inviting more people. See [plans](/plans).

## Audit log

**Roles and permissions → Audit log** lists every change to members, roles, permissions, mail and the workspace itself, newest first, including writes made with API keys.

## Archive, transfer or delete

In **Settings → General**, under **Danger zone**, the owner can:

- **Transfer ownership** to another member. You become an admin.
- **Archive** the workspace. Everyone but the owner is removed and all content becomes read-only.
- **Delete** the workspace. Every file, channel, message and member is permanently deleted. This can't be undone.
